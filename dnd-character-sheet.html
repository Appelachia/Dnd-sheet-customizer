Hit points & survival

Enter final damage after resistance or vulnerability. Temporary HP absorb damage first; replace temporary HP rather than adding them. At 0 HP, resolve damage-related death failures and massive damage with your DM.

Death successesFailures

2024 Exhaustion: −2 to every D20 Test and −5 ft. speed per level; level 6 means death. Both penalties are applied automatically.

Spend Heroic Inspiration to reroll one die immediately and use the new roll. Track its use here; rerolls are resolved manually with the dice roller.

Hit dice & rests

Start either rest with at least 1 HP. Spend Hit Dice during a Short Rest: die + CON, minimum 1 healing. A completed 2024 Long Rest restores all HP and Hit Dice, clears temporary HP, and refreshes rest-based resources. Check the cause before removing Exhaustion.

Defense & conditions

Concentration uses a CON save, DC max(10, half damage rounded down), capped at 30. Speed includes Exhaustion and heavy armor; other movement effects are manual. For Passive Perception, add +5 or −5 in its extra field when advantage or disadvantage applies; both cancel. Conditions are reminders: set relevant advantage, disadvantage, and situational modifiers in the roller. AC uses one calculation at a time. Monk Unarmored Defense excludes shields.

Attacks & damage

Attack bonus = chosen ability + PB when proficient + attack extras. Weapon damage normally adds the same ability modifier; most spell damage does not. Critical damage doubles all dice in the entered damage expression; flat bonuses are added once. Enter range, mastery, and special rules in notes.

Rules reference & how to use this sheet

This sheet uses the revised 2024 fifth edition core rules. Enter your character’s actual choices, spells, equipment, and features. The score builder is not a complete character creator. Class changes do not award features, skills, equipment, or saving throw proficiencies automatically. The first class is your starting class.

Roll buttons use the roller’s current advantage, disadvantage, target, and situational bonus. Advantage and disadvantage cancel each other, regardless of how many sources apply. A total equal to DC or AC succeeds. A natural 20 automatically hits and crits on an attack; a natural 1 automatically misses. Ordinary ability checks and saves have no automatic success or failure on 20 or 1. Death saves use their special rules.

Automatic slots cover core full casters, Paladins, Rangers, and Warlocks. For Eldritch Knights, Arcane Tricksters, Artificers, other supplemental classes, or unusual progressions, choose Manual slots and enter the appropriate table. Set spell abilities and preparation limits per class. Artificer / Custom entries use a configurable Hit Die. Multiclass prerequisites and training are checked against your books by you and your DM.

After changing class levels, Constitution, or HP mode, check current HP. Fixed HP use the maximum Hit Die only at character level 1 and the printed fixed gain thereafter, with a minimum gain of 1 per level. Manual maximum HP are your final total. Long Rest automation assumes an eligible, completed rest; manually restore ability-score or HP-maximum reductions when a rule permits it. Most conditions, resistances, feature-specific rerolls, and exceptions need manual resolution.

Save and backup: browser storage can be cleared or unavailable, especially in file previews. Backup JSON contains all your characters. Import appends characters without replacing existing ones.
